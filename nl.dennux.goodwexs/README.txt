GoodWe XS

Bring your GoodWe XS inverter into Homey and monitor your solar production locally.

Monitor solar production, inverter status, faults and device information directly from your installation.

The app uses Modbus TCP through the GoodWe WiFi/LAN Kit 2.0 and does not depend on external cloud services.

Designed for the GoodWe XS series and validated with the GoodWe 2500-XS.

Requirements

Homey Pro
GoodWe XS inverter
GoodWe WiFi/LAN Kit 2.0

Limitations

Battery systems are not supported.
Other GoodWe models have not been validated.
